## Series bible (applies to every episode; also stored in the Desk under Story → Series guidelines)
- **Formula**: (1) clear picture of the age and time; (2) a problem; (3) real history taught through what characters live; (4) the age's arts, culture, music and its own 1–2 min soundtrack; (5) Egypt or a character who stands for Egypt is reborn with a new opportunity at the end; (6) characters are eyewitnesses and the narrator (the episode's main character) tells their own story and emotions, in English (dubbed later).
- **Images**: each frame an award-winning, ultra-realistic photograph (not painting); framed by nature, symbolic, surreal, supernatural; the consciousness of each age; what it means to be Egyptian whatever race or religion; the Nile present or felt. Characters fully visible. Each age styled in its own art/culture.
- **Motion**: people and animals frozen in time; only ambience moves (water, birds, sand/sandstorm, smoke, light, clothes and hair in the breeze).
- **Craft**: 16:9, ~10 s per frame, 40–60 frames per episode; ~1/3 filler frames (establishing, period-object close-up, ambience, time passing, frozen face, symbolic vision); every frame has real foreground/midground/background depth for the camera move; 23 camera presets incl. drone (fly-forward, rise, orbit, top-down), crane (up/down/over), slide-through. At least one documented codex object every second frame. Easter eggs only when planted on purpose. Title cards only for intro and between chapters.
- **Dynamic camera (27 Nov 2026)**: the owner wants cameras that move THROUGH the frozen moment (walk-throughs, walk-pasts, frozen-time orbits, drone dives/reveals, parallax peeks, FPV). 9 new presets specified in `docs/camera-presets-frozen-time.md` (with Episode 1's per-frame camera plan) are now in the Desk's `CAMERA` (32 presets), with their image rules in `FROZEN_DEPTH`/`depthFor()` and the frozen-time line on every video prompt (local Desk only so far; not yet published to the artifact). On 27 Nov the owner chose to apply the plan to the CURRENT 57-frame Episode 1 by frame number (frames 1–30 and 49–57 from the 66-frame list, 31–48 from the Storm Prayer table; frame 2 "walkPast→orbitFrozen" set to walkPast), so some moves sit on different moments than planned. Before-copy: `<data>\desk\backups\ep-01-nile-breathes.before-camera-plan-2026-11-27.json`. Orbits max 90–180°; these moves need image-to-video AI (start/end frames).
- **Historical accuracy is sacred**: only ancient names before the Greek era (Tjehenu not Libya, Kemet, Wadj-wer, Retjenu, Alashiya…); no gods, objects or names before they are attested; mark reconstructions as such.

## Episode format (EVERY episode, owner's rule, 27 Nov 2026)
1. **Educational intro, 0:00–3:00**: the world of the age (how people lived, ate, dressed, believed, buried their dead, made music), calm and full of real codex objects.
2. **The obstacle, 3:00–5:00**: the problem hits and grows, in sharp contrast with part 1.
3. **Soundtrack climax, 5:00–8:00**: the episode's song (2–3 min, plus an ambient/choir tail if shorter) carries the fight against the obstacle; narration only on a few key lines.
4. **Resolution + education, 8:00–11:00**: rescue and rebirth (Egypt reborn with opportunity) plus a new layer of history about the new life.
About 60–66 frames × 10 s. The narrator is the main character speaking to themselves and the audience, like reading their own diary aloud (a voice device; no writing existed before c. 3200 BC). Not telling the story to a child.
