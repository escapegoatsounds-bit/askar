# Episode 1 · Where the Nile Breathes Life · frame-by-frame guide

*c. 4500 BC · narrator: Nebet (speaks to herself and the audience) · 66 frames × 10 s = 11:00 · 16:9 · (F) = filler*

**Every frame:** ultra-realistic award-winning photograph; people and animals frozen in time; only ambience moves (sand, dust, water, smoke, light, fabric, hair). Each image needs a clear foreground, midground and background for its camera move. Video prompt line for every frame: *"Time is frozen: people and animals are completely still, like a held breath; only dust, sand, water, smoke, light and fabric drift slowly; the camera moves through the still moment."*

**Cast:** Nebet (young: about 20, bronzed olive-brown skin, black braid tied with leather, almond eyes, red-ochre lines on the cheekbones, goatskin wrap, ostrich-eggshell beads; old: grey braid, deeply lined face). Wedja (baby about 1 year, in a leather sling; grown: tall, lean, bronzed, with a fishing net).

## Notes
- **Accuracy:** desert details are Nabta Playa / Gebel Ramlah (c. 4600–4400 BC); river details are Badarian (c. 4400–3900 BC, heartland around el-Badari). The gods are a vision for the audience; Nebet prays without names. The Wadi Sura stencils are older than 4500 BC, so she finds them ancient and adds hers. Haboob walls of 1 km+ and dry lightning in dust storms are real.
- **Song:** about 110 s from frame 31, with the bass drop on frame 44, plus a choir and wind tail to frame 48. Re-time frames once the final mix exists.
- **Effects** (speed ramps, crash zooms, shockwaves, lightning flashes, shake) are added in editing, cut to the drum hits. Orbits stay at 90° (max 180°); walk-throughs, orbits and FPV need image-to-video AI with start and end frames.
