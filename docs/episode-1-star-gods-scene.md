# Episode 1 · The star-gods scene · prompt sets (28 Nov 2026, in the local Desk as frames 41–46)

In the eye of the storm Nebet looks up and sees the gods of the Storm Prayer as constellations, one lighting up on each sung name.

**Placement.** The scene goes right after frame 40 "The eye", under Chorus 2 (rising harmonies, 14 sung lines, 16 gods). Frame 41 "She stands" then becomes her rising out of the vision.

**Length.** At about 5 s a frame the six frames run 30 s, roughly one chorus. At 10 s a frame they run a minute and Chorus 2 plays twice. Either way, frames 41–66 move later.

**Time of day.** The Desk sets the storm at noon. A dust storm can turn noon as dark as night, so the darkness is real; the stars are the vision.

**Payoffs.**
- Hathor is the cow they gave the sky in frame 26.
- Set's stars sit inside the storm wall, setting up frame 43 "Set".
- Sobek rises last, from the river of stars, setting up frame 45 ("the last one rose from water").

## Notes

- **Lyrics.** In the lyrics as pasted, every chorus line after the first has a Hebrew מ (and two lines have נח) where the Arabic م / نح belongs. The copy stored in the Desk is clean, so give the song tool that one.
- **Era.** These gods belong to later Egypt (the Pyramid Texts are c. 2400 BC). As before, the song names them; Nebet doesn't.
- **Hathor.** The idea that Nabta's cattle cult is an early root of Egypt's cow goddesses comes from Fred Wendorf and Romuald Schild, who dug Nabta. It is a suggestion, not proven.
- **The sky.** No real sky shows all of these at once (Orion is a winter sky, the dark rift a summer one). It's a vision.
